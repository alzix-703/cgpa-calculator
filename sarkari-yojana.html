Sarkari Yojana Finder

Check eligibility, benefits, and required documents for major Government Schemes in 2026.

🏛️ PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Get ₹6,000 per year directly into bank account in 3 equal installments for small & marginal farmers.

Documents: Aadhaar, Land Record, Bank Passbook

🏠 PM Awas Yojana (PMAY)

Financial subsidy and assistance for constructing or buying permanent pucca houses.

Documents: Ration Card, Income Certificate, Aadhaar

🏥 Ayushman Bharat Card

Free medical treatment up to ₹5 Lakhs per family per year at empaneled hospitals.

Documents: Ration Card / E-Shram Card, Aadhaar

🔨 PM Vishwakarma Yojana

Collateral-free loans up to ₹3 Lakhs at 5% interest with skill training for traditional artisans.

Documents: Skill Certificate, Aadhaar, Bank Details
